Privacy Policy — Monster Wave: Defense

Effective: October 23, 2026 · App: Monster Wave: Defense (com.jo.sigilstorm) · Developer: Dalrise

This policy explains what information the game Monster Wave: Defense (“the app”) handles. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location, you do not need an account to play, and the app shows no ads.

1. Game data stored on your device

Your game progress (stages, gold, gems, upgrades, bestiary, settings and language) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or choose Settings → Reset progress.

2. In-app purchases

Gem packs are sold through Google Play. Purchases are processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment card or bank details. The app only receives the result of a purchase (such as the product and order status) to deliver the gems.

3. Data sharing

We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Apart from purchase processing by Google Play, the app does not send your data to third parties. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Your choices

  • Delete game data: uninstall the app or use Settings → Reset progress.
  • Purchases are always optional; the whole game can be played without them.

6. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: johyun0218@naver.com
